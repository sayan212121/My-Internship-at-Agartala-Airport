✈️ My Internship at Agartala Airport

By Sayan Debnath

📍 Introduction

Internships are the bridge between academic knowledge and real-world applications. As an Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student, I had the opportunity to intern at Agartala Airport (Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport)—an experience that not only broadened my technical understanding but also taught me vital soft skills and real-life adaptability.

⚙️ What I Worked On

During my internship, I got hands-on exposure to various airport systems and technologies such as:

  • Radar and Communication Systems
  • Surveillance Systems (ADS–B, SSR)
  • Navigation Aids (ILS, DVOR, NDB)
  • Power & Backup Systems
  • CCTV and Fire Alarm Systems

🧠 Technical Skills

  • Real-time Communication Systems
  • System Redundancy Design
  • Ground Navigation Hardware
  • Technical Documentation

🧩 Soft Skills

  • Teamwork & Collaboration
  • Punctuality & Time Management
  • Effective Communication
  • Adaptability

💬 Challenges Faced

  • Technical Jargon Overload
  • Restricted Access Areas
  • Real-World System Complexity

🎯 Final Thoughts

My internship at Agartala Airport was more than just a checkbox on my résumé. It was a transformational learning experience that helped me understand the aviation industry's backbone from a tech perspective...
